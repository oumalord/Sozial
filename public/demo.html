Built for teams and agencies

Start with the organization.
Grow into the workflow.

Each business, brand, or client gets its own workspace, owner, settings, channels, content, and team.

Organization workspace

A dedicated home for a company, agency client, creator, or social media team.

Clear ownership

The organization owner signs in with their own account. Superadmins never set or share their password.

Independent studios

Each workspace can onboard channels, create content, organize media, and plan its own calendar.

Superadmin provisioning

Provision a tenant
in one pass.

01

Create the organization

Enter the business or client name, organization type, owner contact, and starting plan.

02

Assign the owner

Existing accounts receive owner access immediately. New owners are invited by email address and claim the workspace when they register.

03

Hand off the studio

Share the app URL. The owner completes onboarding and begins configuring their organization’s studio.

Content operations

Move from idea
to ready-to-publish.

Compose once

Draft a post, attach approved media, and tailor variants for each selected channel.

Coordinate together

Keep channel accounts, brand assets, teammates, comments, and approvals with the organization.

Schedule with confidence

Use the shared calendar to review timing, spot gaps, and move queued posts across dates.

A calm command center

See the work.
Know what needs attention.

The overview and reporting views use delivery and scheduling data already available in the workspace.

Channel health

Review connected accounts, expired permissions, queued work, and delivery issues.

Publishing calendar

Scan a Monday-first month, week, day, or list view; drag queued posts to reschedule.

Honest reporting

Track confirmed publishes and failures. Audience metrics remain clearly unavailable until a provider supplies them.

2

Create a business workspace, assign an owner email, and choose a plan. Explain that passwords are never set by the admin.

Platform foundation

Ready to operate
on Vercel + Neon.

Vercel serves the app and scheduled publishing jobs. Neon stores tenant and workflow records. Vercel Blob stores shared media assets.

Tenant-aware access

Workspace membership and role checks guard organization data and actions.

Operational visibility

Superadmins can review tenants, plans, system health, failed jobs, and audit activity.

Owner-controlled accounts

Registration and session creation stay with each user; no temporary admin passwords are exposed.
